The English admin was drafted against `docs/admin-glossary.md`, with zh-HK as the source of truth and no zh wording changed except to fix an outright bug. This list collects every decision left to the owner, and the follow-up work deliberately deferred, so that none of it is lost when the scratch workspace is deleted.

How to read it:

- File references are repo-relative, as at the head of `codex/audit-final-sp5a-20261009` (commit `2b317200`). "Around" means the line may have moved a few lines since a report was written.
- "Task n" is the nth of the ten SP-5a tasks. "Final" is the final fix wave (commit `2b317200`).
- Suggested zh appears only where an implementer's report offered one. "—" means none was offered.
- Each item has an id (C, E, I, D, S, F, G, and the Task 9 codes ZE-9-nn) so that you can reply with one decision per item.

## Dates that use the UTC day in zh

In zh, these screens show a stored timestamp by cutting its first ten characters, which is the UTC day. English converts to Hong Kong time. Between 00:00 and 08:00 Hong Kong time the same record can therefore show different days in zh and English. For example, a record stamped `2026-10-06T16:30:00Z` shows 2026-10-06 in zh and 7 Oct 2026 (Wed) in English.
