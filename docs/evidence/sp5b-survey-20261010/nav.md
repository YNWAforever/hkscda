1. **"41 of 50 components have an `h1`; login has none."** Login and reset-password have one (SP-1, `routes/admin/login.tsx:110`, `reset-password.tsx:136`, with tests). 2. **"9 `$id` wrappers that rely on the child component."** There are 7 `$id` route files. They do rely on the child, correctly; the missing piece is the loading, error and not-found states (9 pages).
3. **"Volunteer workspace tab bar (14 items) may need horizontal scroll; group into 3 sub-tabs."** The workspace is a vertical sidebar of **13** pages, already in **2 labelled groups** (日常營運 / 管理員設定), collapsing to a toggle below 1200px, with no horizontal scroll (`volunteerWorkspace.ts:14-28`, `VolunteerAdminShell.tsx:60-100`, `volunteer-workspace.css:258-289`). The 14 is probably 13 plus internships. The AdminLayout tab bar for that group shows 2 tabs (`AdminLayout.tsx:90-93`). The real horizontal-scroll candidates are the 6-tab bars of adoptions and website content.
4. **"義工工作區 holds a further 14 screens."** 13 pages, plus 2 record routes (`people/$id`, `registrations/$id`).
5. **"捐款與助養 is the one group split by role."** Four groups are split by role: donations (supporters is treasurer and admin only), system (statuses admin only; payment methods for all three; access admin only), website content (governance admin only; FAQ not for treasurer) and volunteers (policy pages admin only). Source: `lib/admin/access.ts:45-91,93-119`; I computed the visible groups per role.
6. **"Collapsed sidebar shows icons with tooltips: Done."** Native `title` only, missing on the Task link and the collapse toggle, no keyboard or touch tooltip (N4).
7. **"One active item is always visible in both: Done."** True for every route that renders `AdminLayout` except `access-denied`; `adoption-guides` renders no layout at all (N1).
8. **"Breadcrumb: Group / destination only."** True for AdminLayout, but there are three breadcrumb implementations with different grammars (global 2-level; animals 後台 / 動物管理 / tab; volunteer shell brand / page / generic detail title), and the volunteer pages show two at once.
9. **Template "Used by" column.** List: "people" is a card list with a hand-rolled pager, not a `DataTable`. Editor: no autosave exists anywhere; guide releases and content have version and gate; payment methods is a CMS workflow. Settings: statuses is a master-detail list, volunteer policy and daily quotas are draft-to-publish editors, access is a table plus audit log; none has per-card Save plus audit footnote (only adoption instructions shows "last updated by").
10. **Maintainability rule 3** (register in `adminNav.ts` and `access.ts` with a test) is met for the 25 nav ids, but 10 volunteer pages are registered only in `volunteerWorkspace.ts`, and no test ties routes to the nav (M3).
11. **LoadFailure "with the HTTP status class in plain words."** `LoadFailure.tsx:93-98` shows a heading, guidance and a hash reference only.
12. Audit "mobile drawer focuses the page `h1`": implemented (`AdminLayout.tsx:234-251`), but it finds nothing on pages whose `h1` appears only after load.
