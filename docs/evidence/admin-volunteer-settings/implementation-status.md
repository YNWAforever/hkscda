## Runtime behavior that operators must understand

- The latest effective assessment policy controls promotion. verified_attendance evaluates the Nth immutable attendance fact; monthly_assessment evaluates only the fixed completed month. Newcomer-to-regular promotion writes profile history and audit evidence. Monthly reconciliation also catches eligible retained history under a verified-attendance policy, with a distinct reconciliation event; mismatched assessment scopes are rejected.
- Senior requirements use verified join date, history coverage, configured tenure and regular-observation duration. Meeting them creates an immutable candidate for staff review; it never auto-promotes.
- Unknown history is not zero attendance. Completed assessment months retain their first policy version; reruns deduplicate person/period/type notifications.
- Only configured channels can be published for live delivery. Email requires the configured transport; WhatsApp currently has no dispatcher and is rejected for live use. Dry-run may exercise the local sink without sending.
- Provider acceptance is not delivery. Delivery requires callback/provider evidence; failed durable jobs may be retried through the audited staff workflow.
- Runtime jobs require the explicitly configured, active administrator authority. Missing or revoked authority fails closed.
